How faithful is the dream?

The model compresses each frame into 64 numbers, and a recurrent network predicts how those numbers change when you press a key. It never sees the map directly: what lies beyond the edge of the screen, which coins are gone and where the slimes patrol all have to live in its memory. The longer you walk without a real frame, the more it can drift.
