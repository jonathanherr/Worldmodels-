Squares in Squares Bench

Pack n unit squares into the smallest square box. Squeeze the walls and let the contact physics push, slide and tilt the squares; drag any square to help it out. The record entries load the public record packings with physics off, so you can select a whole block and turn it by tenths of a degree.

drag: move · wheel or Q/E: rotate (shift: finer/coarser) · B: select block · Esc: one square · arrows: nudge · Tab: next square · S: squeeze · G: grow
